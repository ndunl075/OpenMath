Eight more were downloaded and discarded: a diffusion-model loss, a complex
analysis series, a partial-fraction identity holding three expressions on one
line, and a page of a Chinese networking paper. None could be labelled without
guessing, and a wrong ground truth is worse than a missing one — it turns a
correct reading into a recorded failure.
